# Adventure playtest, 7 September 2026

Version 0.23.0 was exercised against DF 0.53.16 / DFHack 53.16-r1.1 under
CrossOver. Control used structured state and native inputs throughout.

The character is Athis Pridesings, unit 8525. The user had already killed
Windgale, the Utahraptor, when this play session began. Comparing the last
pre-kill snapshot with the next saved character report gives +52 Hammer,
+55 Fighter, +18 Armor, +3 Dodging and +2 Observation XP. Tracking increased
41 XP over that interval. Those snapshots span manual play and cannot isolate
the killing blow or exclude another fight between readings. The evidence is
`.df-llm/raptor-head-round-after-brief.json` and `.df-llm/burden-before.json`.

Subsequent harness play killed one wild turkey and completed movement training.
The turkey hunt gained 10 Hammer, 10 Fighter, 6 Armor and 62 Tracking XP, plus
2 Endurance points. The training circuits reached Novice Sneak and Skilled
Tracking. After the final receipt trial, Sneak was 100/600 toward Adequate and
Tracking was 72/900 toward Proficient. Hammer remained Novice at 112/600;
Fighter remained Novice at 139/600. Strength stayed 1101, so this training did
not increase carrying capacity.

The player returned to local tile (69, 64, 131), standing with sneaking off,
full blood, no wounds and no exhaustion. Backpack, armor, hammer and shield
were restored. Carried mass is 95.46225 kg against a 63.09 kg native burden
threshold, still Overburdened. Save and continue completed under
`leveling-2026-09-07-v023`.

The playtest led to these changes:

- Concise unit inspection includes healthy body-part IDs, allowing a strike
  without another full anatomy query.
- Confirmed corpses no longer block local walking. Unknown and living
  occupants retain the controller's existing constraints.
- Completion dispatches replan after bounded native stale-state rejections
  that explicitly prove no input was sent. Fresh interruption conditions are
  checked first; uncertain transport failures are never replayed.
- Pickup refusals include the native no-free-grasp reason and held item IDs.
  Equipment disposition remains the controller's decision.
- Completed equipment sequences retain the burden reader for their final
  assessment. A live stow/wield sequence verified the Overburdened result.
- The controller can request up to 1,024 inputs; the default remains 32.
  A 521-input training sequence stopped after 182 inputs when a new spearman
  appeared, then completed through an explicit resume without repeating stages.
- Receipts include native XP deltas, rank changes, progress toward the next
  rank and changed stored attributes. The narrow sample shares the character
  reader's accessors, avoids other profiles, and adds no RPCs. Resumes count
  each interval once. Missing samples and identity changes remain explicit.
- Dispatch time budgets now cover synchronous RPC waits. The initial save
  exceeded the old 10-second RPC timeout but completed in the game; recovery
  verified it with zero input. A subsequent save completed in 19.6 seconds
  using `--seconds 90` alone. Its receipt included verified file evidence.

Metrics are in `.df-llm/play-metrics.jsonl`, with native traces in
`.df-llm/leveling-trace.jsonl`. Comparable short training trials show:

| Measurement | Before XP receipts | With XP receipts |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Movement steps | 40 | 40 |
| Native inputs, including sneak toggles | 41 | 42 |
| Harness duration | 20.10 s | 21.15 s |
| Receipt output tokens, o200k_base | 274 | 310 |
| Follow-up character-read tokens | 3,881 | 0 |
| Combined output tokens | 4,155 | 310 |
| RPCs | 178 | 183 |

The added final sneak toggle makes these close comparisons, not identical
workloads. The receipt change removes a follow-up query and about 92.5% of the
controller output tokens in this example; it does not establish a movement
speed improvement. Movement still uses about 4.35 RPCs per input. Ten short
read-only RPC samples per transport mode produced overlapping latency ranges
with fresh and reused connections, so no connection-pool change was made.

The hunt episode spent 41.5 seconds inside the harness and 702.8 seconds outside
it, including development, inspection and controller work. Those gaps are not
proof of LLM thinking time. Two qualifying bounces exposed mechanics that
needed fixes. The later training interruption was the requested visibility
policy working as intended.

Offline tests initially inherited live controller settings and wrote 92 fake
interaction records. Those records and their child RPCs are preserved in
`.df-llm/metrics-fixtures-accidental.jsonl`, outside the play measurements.
`python -m tests.run` now isolates settings and disables play metrics; Makefile
and CI use that runner.

Validation: the full check passed with 409 Python tests; 235 isolated native
fixtures passed with zero game inputs. Live receipts verified XP gains, corpse
traversal, large-budget interruption/resume, equipment burden and save recovery.
Saved evidence includes `leveling-checks.txt`, `leveling-native-checks.json`,
`leveling-after-hunt.json`, `leveling-trained.json` and
`leveling-transport-probe.json` under `.df-llm`.
